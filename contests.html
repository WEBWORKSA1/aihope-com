Challenge FAQ

Does it cost anything to enter the AI Hope Challenge?

No. Entry is free and no purchase or donation is required. Donations to the Hope Fund never affect judging.

Can I enter with work I created using AI?

Yes, that is the point of the challenge. Tell us which tools you used and what you did yourself. The entry must be your own project and must not copy other people's work.

When are winners announced?

Finalists are contacted after entries close on December 15, 2026. Winners are announced by January 15, 2027, on this page and in the weekly brief.
