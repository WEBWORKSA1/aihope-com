# AiHope.com: concept choice and niche research

Researched October 2026.

## 1. Concept choice

| Concept | Search demand | AdSense RPM | Approval risk | Lead value | Competition | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI tools directory | High | Medium | Low | Medium (paid listings) | Saturated: TAAFT, Futurepedia, Toolify, FutureTools | Reject as the core; keep as a section |
| AI-for-good nonprofit hub | Low | Low | Low | Low | ITU AI for Good, AI4Good, Partnership on AI | Reject as the core; keep as hope stories |
| AI mental-health and wellbeing support | Medium | Medium | High (health is YMYL, sensitive ads) | Low | Clinical providers | Reject |
| **AI careers: “future-proof your work”** | **High and recurring** | **High (education, careers, finance advertisers)** | **Low with trust pages** | **High (course, bootcamp and coaching leads; B2B)** | **Fragmented; leaders lack plans and lead capture** | **Chosen** |

**Why the chosen concept wins**

- **Demand and urgency.** The World Economic Forum projects 170M jobs created and 92M displaced by 2030, and 22% of today's jobs disrupted. The IMF estimates that about 40% of global jobs are exposed to AI, rising to about 60% in advanced economies. The ILO puts it at 1 in 4 workers globally. Every one of those workers is a potential reader with a personal question.
- **Monetizable intent.** PwC found a 56% wage premium for jobs requiring AI skills. Coursera's generative-AI enrollments grew 195% year over year to 8M+. People are actively spending to reskill, which is exactly who course, bootcamp and career advertisers pay to reach.
- **Brand fit.** “Hope” is the emotional answer to AI job anxiety, so the name works as a positioning statement.
- **Gap.** willrobotstakemyjob.com has no lead capture. replacedbyrobot.info shows no methodology. TAAFT's Job Impact Index sits behind a login. None of them turns a risk score into a personal plan, and that plan is the lead magnet.

**Planning assumptions (not measured, validate after launch):**
- Career and education pages commonly earn above-average AdSense RPM in tier-1 countries.
- Education lead referrals typically pay per qualified lead or by revenue share.
- Measure actual RPM, funnel conversion and partner payouts in the first 90 days before scaling content.

**Revenue streams built in:** AdSense (auto ads plus reserved slots), learning-partner leads (opt-in), B2B team readiness inquiries, paid listings and sponsorships, newsletter sponsorship, contest and scholarship sponsors, Hope Fund donations, and inquiries about acquiring the website or domain through the partner bar.

## 3. How the findings shaped AiHope.com

- **SEO:** one page per occupation with a task breakdown, safer adjacent careers, skills, tools and FAQ schema. A published methodology. A jobs A–Z hub. Topic guides built on the edX layout: key takeaways, table of contents, tables, FAQ.
- **AdSense:** about, editorial policy, privacy (with Google's advertising disclosures), terms, disclaimer and contact pages. Substantial original text on every page. Ads kept away from buttons and forms. Sponsored placements labeled.
- **Lead conversion:** questions first and email last, five steps with a progress bar, instant on-screen value, consent plus an optional partner opt-in, optional phone number, trust lines, an email-my-result form on the checker, an exit-intent checklist and a sticky mobile CTA.
- **Donations:** monthly default, presets with the second one selected, a custom amount, live button text, impact framing, a published allocation and an honest note that gifts are not tax-deductible.
- **Community:** a contest with prize, deadline and countdown cards on the HeroX model; separate paths for volunteers and partners; reader story submissions.
