Questions about giving

Is my donation tax-deductible?

No. AI Hope is an independent educational publication, not a registered charity, so gifts are not tax-deductible. If we form a charitable entity in future, we will say so clearly on this page.

How do I pay?

Choose an amount and press Give. You will be taken to a secure payment page, or, while our payment links are being set up, we email you a secure link to complete your gift. We never ask for card details on this website.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Yes, at any time, through the link in your receipt or by contacting us through the contact form.
