About your plan

Is the AI career plan really free?

Yes. The plan, the checker and all learning paths are free. We are funded by advertising, partners and reader donations. If you opt in, we may introduce you to vetted course providers, coaches or employers that fit your plan; you can opt out at any time.

What happens to my information?

We use it to send your plan and the weekly brief. We never sell your personal data. Partner introductions only happen if you tick the optional box. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How is the plan created?

Your answers are matched to our job data (task exposure, skills and adjacent roles) and to a curated list of reputable courses. The roadmap adapts to your goal, your comfort with AI and the hours you have each week.
