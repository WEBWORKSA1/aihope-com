Work with AI Hope

We are a small, remote team building the most useful free resource for people whose work is changing because of AI. We're looking for people who care about that mission and are good at what they do.

Open roles

Most roles start as freelance or volunteer and grow into paid positions as the Hope Fund grows. All roles are remote.

Freelance · paid per article

Contributing writer, AI & careers

Write practical, well-sourced guides on AI at work. Experience in HR, careers or tech journalism is a plus.

Volunteer

Career mentor

Give one hour a month to coach someone changing careers. Recruiters, coaches and hiring managers welcome.

Freelance

Expert reviewer

Review occupation reports in your field: health, law, finance, education, trades or engineering. Named credit on every page you review.

Contract

Front-end developer

Improve our interactive tools in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Accessibility and performance matter here.

Freelance

Video creator

Produce short explainers for our YouTube channel: AI tools, career stories and how-tos.

Volunteer → paid

Community & social lead

Grow our newsletter and social channels, run the AI Hope Challenge community and spotlight reader stories.

Apply or introduce yourself

Tell us what you'd like to do and share a link to your work. We reply to every application within two weeks.

  • Remote, flexible hours
  • Real impact on people's careers
  • Credit and portfolio pieces for everything you make
  • Priority for paid roles as funding grows

We welcome applicants of every background and career stage, including people transitioning out of roles affected by AI.
