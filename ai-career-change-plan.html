A 90-day plan to change careers in the age of AI

Ninety days won't make you an expert. It can take you from “worried and stuck” to “clear target, new skills, first proof and real conversations”. Here is the plan, week by week.

By the AI Hope Editorial TeamUpdated October 10, 20269 min read

Key takeaways

  • Aim for an adjacent role that reuses most of your experience.
  • Learn with free courses first; pay only for credentials employers ask for.
  • One visible project beats three certificates.
  • Talk to people in the target role early, not at the end.

Before you start: three decisions

  1. Time: how many hours a week can you protect? Five to eight is enough for this plan.
  2. Money: how many months of expenses could you cover during a transition? If it's less than three, plan to change while employed.
  3. Direction: score your current job in the checker and shortlist two adjacent roles from its report.

Days 1–30: Explore and build foundations

WeekFocusActions
1Choose a targetRead the reports for your two shortlisted roles. Collect ten real job postings and list common requirements.
2Talk to peopleMessage five people in the target role (use the networking prompt). Aim for two 15-minute conversations.
3AI foundationsStart Elements of AI or AI Fluency. Use an AI assistant for one real task every day.
4DecidePick one target role based on what you heard. Write down the three skills you most need to prove.

Days 31–60: Learn the core skill and apply it

WeekFocusActions
5–6Core courseTake one course in your target role's most important skill. See learning paths.
7Start a projectChoose a small, real project that shows the skill: an analysis, an automation, a redesigned process, a lesson plan.
8Use it at workApply the new skill in your current job, even in a small way. Record the result in numbers if possible.

Days 61–90: Prove it and go to market

WeekFocusActions
9Finish and publishComplete the project and write a one-page case study: problem, approach, result, what you'd do next.
10RebrandRewrite your resume and LinkedIn headline for the target role, leading with the project.
11Apply selectivelySend five tailored applications and follow up with the people you met in month one.
12Interview practiceRun three AI mock interviews and one with a friend. Plan your next 90 days.

Examples of realistic moves

  • Administrative assistant → project coordinator: add a project-management course, automate a team workflow and document the time saved.
  • Bookkeeper → financial analyst or client advisor: learn spreadsheet modeling with AI and build a cash-flow forecast for a real small business.
  • Customer service rep → customer success: improve a knowledge base or chatbot answers and show a drop in repeat tickets.
  • Copywriter → content strategist: build a content plan backed by search data and original interviews.

If you're mid-career or older

Experience is an advantage in an AI transition: you know how work really gets done, where the risks are and how to handle people. Lead with that judgment and pair it with visible AI skills. Mid-career changers often move faster than beginners because they can combine a new tool with deep domain knowledge.

Frequently asked questions

Can I really change careers in 90 days?

You can complete the groundwork in 90 days: choosing a target, learning the core skill, building proof and starting applications. Landing the new role may take longer, especially for bigger jumps, which is why adjacent moves are recommended.

Do I need to quit my job to retrain?

Usually not. Five to eight focused hours a week is enough for this plan, and applying new skills in your current job gives you the strongest proof.
