Editorial policy

How we research, write, review and correct everything on AI Hope.

Accuracy and sources

We base factual claims on primary or authoritative sources: international organizations (such as the ILO, IMF, OECD and World Economic Forum), government statistics agencies, peer-reviewed research, and official documentation from tool makers. We link to sources where readers can check them, and we date our content so you know how current it is.

Exposure scores

Job exposure scores are editorial estimates produced with a consistent, published methodology. They are reviewed at least twice a year and whenever a major change in AI capabilities makes a rating out of date.

Use of AI in our work

We use AI tools the way we recommend readers use them: to help with research, outlines, summaries and first drafts. Every page is reviewed, edited and fact-checked by a person before publication, and a person is accountable for what we publish.

Independence

Advertising, sponsorship and partnerships are handled separately from editorial decisions. Sponsored placements are labeled. Partners cannot buy a better exposure score, a higher ranking or favorable coverage. Tools are listed because they are useful, not because they pay.

Expert review

We are building a network of practitioners to review occupation reports in their fields. Reviewed pages will name the reviewer and the date of review. Interested? See careers and volunteering.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through the contact form with the page address. We correct factual errors promptly and note significant corrections on the page.

Not professional advice

Our content is general information for education. It is not individual career, legal, financial or medical advice. For decisions with significant consequences, consult a qualified professional.
